You've chosen to open the coffin!

You step forward a sword in hand and open the coffin! It’s empty! But just then the
door to the room opens and the vampire lord himself steps in, surprise evident on his
face. Already prepared to fight, you launch forward towards the vampire lord, and your
party quickly follows suit. The vampire lord tries to put up a fight, but it’s one against
four and you make quick work of him.

You are no fool, and know burning the body is the best way to make sure the vampire lord
is truly done for. Your party is able to start a fire in the basement, and get out the way
you came before anyone else who might be in the manor can catch you. You and your companion
make it a safe distance away and watch as the fire spreads to the rest of the vampire lord’s
manor, freeing the village of his evil for good.

THE END.
